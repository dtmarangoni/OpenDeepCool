# Spectrum test signals

48 000 samples per second, one channel (both alike); a tick is 4800 samples, and the window of tick n (from 0) is the last 2048 samples of its 100 ms: samples 4800(n + 1) − 2048 to 4800(n + 1) − 1. A tone's sample n, counted from the signal's first sample, is A · sin(2π f n / 48 000) with A = 10^((dBFS + moved) / 20), computed in f64 and stored as f32; tones on one line add. Noise: x ← (1103515245 x + 12345) mod 2³¹ from x = 1 at the signal's start, the sample (x ÷ 2³⁰ − 1) · A. `moved` is the dB a signal was moved by so that no value it draws lies within 10⁻⁶ of a rounding boundary.
